The story

Every skyline
has its icons.

Some landmarks are grand. Others become familiar in their own quiet way.

Zaeem Tower is celebrated here for its unmistakable water-tower form — the tank above, the structure below, and the silhouette that makes it easy to recognise.

This is an independent visual tribute: a place to give the tower a name, a little space, and a story that can grow with the people who know it.
